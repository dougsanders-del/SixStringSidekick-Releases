Six String Sidekick 0.2.0 (26)

New in builds 25 and 26

  • Guitar or Bass, throughout the app: switch on Home, from the instrument control at the top of tool screens, or from the Mac Instrument menu. Bass supports 4, 5 or 6 strings, bass tunings, correctly sized fretboards and tab staves, and root, fifth, octave and arpeggio positions in Chord Finder.
  • Microphone tuner: guitar and bass tuning presets, including bass Drop D, with pitch detection down to low B.
  • Chord Progression Builder: choose a key and style, tap chords to build a progression, use Suggest for familiar patterns, and see what fits next. Transpose, view capo shapes, loop playback at 60–180 bpm, copy the progression, or save it into a song. Works with Guitar or Bass.
  • Updated offline Help and User Manual cover both new tools and the instrument switch. Existing songs stay unchanged; the app starts on Guitar.

Requires macOS 14 or newer. Universal for Apple Silicon and Intel. This personal test build is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. Microphone tuning and progression playback have automated coverage but still need real-instrument and speaker listening checks.
